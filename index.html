Common Prepositions - حروف الجر الشائعة

Place & Time - مكان وزمان
In
في / داخل
When to use - متى تستخدم:
  • Inside something closed (rooms, buildings, boxes)
  • Months, years, seasons, centuries
  • Parts of the day (in the morning)
  • Countries and cities
"The cat is in the box"
القطة في الصندوق
"I was born in 2000"
ولدت في عام 2000
Place & Time - مكان وزمان
On
على
When to use - متى تستخدم:
  • Touching a surface (table, wall, floor)
  • Days of the week (on Monday)
  • Dates (on June 5th)
  • Special days (on Christmas)
"The book is on the table"
الكتاب على الطاولة
"The meeting is on Monday"
الاجتماع يوم الاثنين
Place - مكان
Under
تحت
When to use - متى تستخدم:
  • Below something, covered by it
  • Less than a number or age
  • Controlled or governed by
"The dog is under the bed"
الكلب تحت السرير
"Children under 5 get free tickets"
الأطفال تحت سن 5 يحصلون على تذاكر مجانية
Place - مكان
Behind
خلف
When to use - متى تستخدم:
  • At the back of something
  • Supporting someone secretly
  • Responsible for something (behind the idea)
"The car is behind the house"
السيارة خلف المنزل
"Who is behind this project?"
من وراء هذا المشروع؟
Time - زمان
At
في (للوقت الدقيق)
When to use - متى تستخدم:
  • Exact time (at 5 o'clock)
  • Specific points (at noon, at night)
  • Addresses with numbers (at 123 Main St)
  • Events (at the party, at work)
"I wake up at 7 o'clock"
أستيقظ في الساعة السابعة
"She is at school now"
هي في المدرسة الآن
Direction - اتجاه
To
إلى
When to use - متى تستخدم:
  • Movement toward a place
  • Giving something to someone
  • Time (ten to five = 4:50)
  • Comparisons (prefer A to B)
"I go to school every day"
أذهب إلى المدرسة كل يوم
"Give this letter to your teacher"
أعطِ هذه الرسالة إلى معلمك
Purpose - غرض
For
لـ / لأجل
When to use - متى تستخدم:
  • Purpose or reason (for studying)
  • Duration (for two hours)
  • Intended recipient (for you)
  • Exchange (pay $5 for coffee)
"This gift is for you"
هذه الهدية لك
"I studied for three hours"
درست لمدة ثلاث ساعات
Topic - موضوع
About
حول / عن
When to use - متى تستخدم:
  • Subject or topic of discussion
  • Approximately (about 10 people)
  • Concerned with (worried about)
  • Doing something (busy about)
"We talked about the lesson"
تحدثنا عن الدرس
"There were about 50 students"
كان هناك حوالي 50 طالباً
Company - مصاحبة
With
مع
When to use - متى تستخدم:
  • Accompanied by someone
  • Using a tool (cut with a knife)
  • Having a quality (a man with courage)
  • Agreement (agree with you)
"I study with my friend"
أدرس مع صديقي
"Write with a pen"
اكتب بقلم
